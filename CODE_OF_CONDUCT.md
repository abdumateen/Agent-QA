# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Agent QA welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or role.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, constructive,
and safe community.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Respect different perspectives and experiences.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm.
- Protect private information shared in confidence.
- Follow project documentation and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately hostile remarks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention.
- Unwelcome sexual advances or contact.
- Threats of violence or encouraging harm.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, reviews, or project infrastructure.
- Trolling, baiting, or sustained bad-faith participation.
- Unwanted contact after a clear request to stop.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions,
chat channels, events, and any setting where a person represents Agent QA.

Maintainers may also address behavior outside project spaces when it materially
affects the safety or ability of community members to participate.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
project maintainers through the repository's private security or maintainer
contact channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public
issue.

A report should include:

- A description of what happened.
- The date and location.
- Names or account identifiers of people involved, when known.
- Links or other supporting material.
- Any immediate safety concerns.
- Whether you prefer follow-up contact.

Maintainers will handle reports confidentially where practical. They will
acknowledge reports, review the available information, and take appropriate
action. Reports made in good faith will not result in retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. A private request to change behavior.
2. A warning.
3. Temporary restriction from project spaces.
4. Removal of comments or contributions.
5. Temporary or permanent removal from the community.
6. Referral to the relevant platform or authorities when necessary.

The response will consider the circumstances, severity, impact, history, and
available evidence.

## Appeals

A person affected by an enforcement decision may request a review from a
different maintainer or maintainer group when one is available. Appeals should
explain why the decision or its severity should be reconsidered.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Making the code of conduct easy to find.
- Responding to reports promptly and respectfully.
- Protecting reporter privacy where possible.
- Applying standards consistently.
- Documenting enforcement decisions appropriately.
- Avoiding conflicts of interest during investigations.
